## 2026-10-24: Fighting Styles piece 2 (P3.B2)

The second of three pieces of P3.B2, built in `engine_v3` from `docs/specs/boarding.md` and `docs/specs/fighting-styles.md`: the deck fight, the boarder's edge, Drill, Fire Pots, the Boarder and the Firebrand, Hot Shot, fire, overtime, the last two templates, and the Grappled rule of Old Shellback. Piece 3 reworks Old Shellback, writes the replay and Gazette text and rewrites GAMEPLAY. The deck rules are in `combat/deck.ts`, the fire rules in `combat/fire.ts`, the trait rules in `combat/styles.ts`, and the rolls are drawn in `battle.ts` and `fleet.ts`, each from its own labelled stream. Where the spec is silent the prototype on `proto-fighting-styles` was followed, and where the two disagree the spec won. **Numbers that come from the prototype and not the spec** (`tools/proto/tune_state_v7.json` has no override of any of them, and no script in `tools/proto` changes them): `combat.deck.hit` and `guard` for Charge (1.5, 1.25), Volley (1.1, 1.0), Repel (0.6, 0.6), Fire Pots guard (1.0) and Fists (1.0, 1.0); `fireChance` 0.3 and `fireHull` 8; `drill.perLevel` [0.04, 0.06, 0.08]; `drill.secondWaveShare` 0.25. **Numbers with no source at all**, taken as placeholders: `holdSpace.firePots` 0.5 (the other supplies) and `supplies.firePotPrice` 8 (P5 sets the prices; nothing reads it). Nothing was tuned. The wheel, measured on the real engine with all six templates, equal fresh sloops, Drill 0, both seats, nine waters and 1800 battles a pair, lands inside the spec's bands for all 15 pairs and within 3 points of its measured table for 12 of them (see D180). One thing the first measurements of Drill found is not inside any line: D181.

| D160 | **The deck fight.** The Grappled column of the range table and `combat.boarding.repel` are gone, so the great guns fire nothing while Grappled: no damage, no luck roll, no Powder. `GunsOrder` gains `hotShot`, `charge`, `volley` and `firePots` (Repel and Board were there), and a new `DeckOrder` (Charge, Volley, Repel, Fire Pots, Fists) says what a crew really fights with. | D161 | **What a crew fights with.** In the round the ships grapple only a crew whose own Board made the grapple, or joined it because both Boarded, fights as a Charge. | D165 | **Drill is the sixth `Stat`.** `Ship.levels.drill` starts at 0 (`zeroLevels`), is read through `drillOf` with a default of 0 for a ship saved before Drill, and is copied into `BattleShip.drill` only when it is above 0. | D169 | **The Firebrand.** Hot Shot is the `hotShot` Guns order: Hull, by the `hot` row of the range table, `hotShotPowder` (2) times the Powder of a round of guns, using up what is left when there is less, and out of Powder it fires at the out of Powder factor like any gun. `playableOrder` makes it Round shot for any other ship, and the record keeps `asked` (D146). | D170 | **Fire in the battle.** Fires are kept for the battle, by ship (the rounds left and the ship that lit it), and are not part of `BattleShip`; a fire burns out with the battle and carries into no later battle or Tide. A fire lit in a round burns at the end of that round, so a 2 round fire does 2 hits and a 3 round fire 3. A burning ship loses `fireLoss` Hull: 12, 60% of that (7) for a Gunline (fire drill) and twice that (24) for a Firebrand (the pitch in its hold), never more than it has. The order at the end of a round is the guns, the deck fight, the Fire Pots (thrown, used up, flashed) and the embers, then the fire damage of every burning ship, then the Sunk, Captured and Escaped checks. A burning ship that is Grappled still burns, a fire can sink a ship and a Sunk ship is towed as always. A ship the fire sinks in the round a Capture would have happened is Sunk, because Sunk is checked first. A new fire on a burning ship keeps the longer, and the one already there on a tie. Fire Hull counts as damage dealt by the other side for Victory on points and is credited to the ship that lit the fire for the loot split. A round that ends in an Escape still burns; a Struck Colours round stops everything, fire too | Fighting styles spec section 3 and edge cases, D138 | P3.B2 |

## 2026-10-24: Fighting Styles piece 1 (P3.B2)
