# How to play Rumtide (Season 0)

You captain a small ship in the Brine, a warm archipelago of rum ports and reefs. Once a day you give your ship orders. Every evening at Tide time (21:00 unless the game master says otherwise) the **Tide** turns: everyone's orders play out at once, and the **Gazette** tells the story. Two minutes a day is enough.

Season 0 is a two week beta of 14 Tides. You start with a sloop and 120 doubloons.

## Your day

1. **Gazette tab.** Read what happened at the last Tide. Open any battle to see it round by round.
2. **Port tab** (when you are docked). Sell cargo, buy cargo, repair, buy upgrades, or feed the Shipwright towards a bigger hull.
3. **Orders tab.** Pick where to sail, what you are up to, and how to fight if trouble finds you. Press **Ready**. When every captain is Ready the Tide can turn early.

You can change your orders until the Tide turns. Pressing Save without Ready keeps them but does not count you as Ready.

## Money

* **Chest:** doubloons that are safe. You spend them in any port.
* **Plunder and cargo** (Rum, Sugar, Spice): carried on your ship, and the only things you can lose. Plunder is banked when you dock.
* Each port makes one good cheaply and pays well for another. Sail goods from where they are cheap to where they are wanted. Selling a lot lowers your own price, so two captains on the same route earn less.
* Upgrades and bigger hulls are never lost. The Brigantine is the first big goal.

## Sailing

* Ports, islands, reefs and wrecks are joined by sea lanes. Your **speed** is how many hops you can sail in one Tide (2 for a sloop).
* A **docked ship cannot be attacked.** A ship you leave in port stays safe.
* Only a **Hunter** starts a fight. Intents:
  * **Trade Run:** does not start fights.
  * **Sail Quietly:** better chance to slip away, but no hunting or exploring.
  * **Hunt:** fight merchants, players, anyone, or one named captain.
  * **Explore:** sail to an island or wreck and draw an event (treasure, a repair, a reef).
  * **Escort:** follow a fleet mate and join their battles.

## Fighting

A battle is 3 rounds. Before the Tide you secretly pick a maneuver for each round:

**Broadside beats Board. Board beats Outmaneuver. Outmaneuver beats Broadside.**

Each maneuver uses a different stat (Cannons, Crew, Sails). Winning a round doubles your damage and halves theirs. Using the same maneuver twice in a row hits 20% weaker, so do not be predictable. Every battle is public, so other captains can study your habits.

You can set a **Parley**: if a hunter is 25% or 50% stronger than you (your choice), you hand over 10% of your plunder and cargo and sail on instead of fighting. If you lose a fight you lose part of what you carry, never your upgrades or your ship.

## Staying fair

* Beating a much weaker ship pays little, and hunting weaker ships puts a **bounty** on you.
* After you lose a fight to another player, nobody can hunt you for 2 Tides.
* Week 1 is **Calm Seas**: loot from player fights is halved. Week 2 is full strength.

## Missing a day

Nothing bad happens. Your ship follows your **standing orders**: it stays docked, or sails quietly to the nearest port, and your standing battle plan defends it. Each missed Tide also stores **Shore Leave** (up to 3): a bonus of 50% on your income when you come back.

## Fleets and the score

* Join or create a **fleet** on the Fleet tab. Fleet mates see each other's orders, earn more when they end the Tide close together, and hit harder in the same battle.
* A **treasure galleon** sails on Tides 7 and 14. A lone ship never beats it, but two or three ships can, and the prize is worth more when shared. Sail it home to a port to bank it.
* Your score is **Legend**: profit from trade and plunder, fights won, and taking part in a galleon hunt. The Legend tab ranks everyone. The leader is marked **Most Wanted**, and beating them pays extra.
* Captains far behind the median get a small income bonus (**Fair Winds**), so catching up is always possible.
* When the last Tide has turned the season is over. The app stays open to read: the Legend tab becomes the **final ranking** with every score shown, and the Gazette keeps every Tide. Orders, Port and Fleet are closed.
