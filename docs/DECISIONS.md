# Decisions

What was decided, when and why. Newest first. When a design decision changes, add an entry here in the same commit.

## How to use the decisions files

This file holds only the newest work, so it can be read in one go. Older entries are in `docs/decisions/` and count as this file in the order of authority:

| File | What | Ids |
|-|-|-|
| `docs/decisions/p3-styles-built.md` | Fighting Styles piece 3, the simulator learns the styles (P3.B2, P3.B3), 24 October 2026 | D182 to D215 |
| `docs/decisions/p3-styles-design.md` | Fighting Styles pieces 1 and 2, the prototype, the boarding rework (P3.B, P3.B1, P3.B2), 20 to 24 October 2026 | D116 to D181 |
| `docs/decisions/v03-core-to-p3-1.md` | The v0.3 engine core, combat, doctrines, encounters and the simulator (P0, P1, P2, P3.1), 18 to 19 October 2026 | D1 to D115 |
| `docs/decisions/v02.md` | The v0.2 prototype, days 2 to 7 and client steps 1 to 8, 14 to 15 October 2026 | no ids |

* To find a decision by its id, search for it (for example `grep -rn "| D142 " docs/DECISIONS.md docs/decisions`) and read only that part. Do not read a whole archive file to find one row.
* Add new entries at the top of this file, below this section. Never add to an archive file.
* Keep every table row under 2,000 characters, because longer lines are cut off when read. Split a long row into a second row with the id and the word continued.
* When this file grows past about 40 KB, move its oldest sections into a new file in `docs/decisions/` and add a row to the table above.

**Findings for P3.2b.** Every single number is a cliff for check 5, on the quick run: `clawHands` 52 to 104 makes every style 0%, to 26 makes every style 100%; `armour` 0.8 to 0.6 leaves Gunline 53% and Firebrand 54% but drops the Skirmisher to 7% while the Boarder stays 99%; `hull` 600 drops the Bombardier to 16%; `retreatHull` 0.7 lifts the Bombardier to 76% and the Rammer to 72%; `retreatClaws` 0.125 leaves the Boarder at 89%, the Firebrand at 95% and the Rammer at 43%; `cannons` 44 drops the Gunline and the Firebrand to 34% and the Boarder to 95%; `helm` 20 gives Gunline 85%, Rammer 100%, Boarder 100%; `mortarFactor` moves only the Bombardier (16% at 1.5, 75% at 6). So the answer is a combination, not a step. The Boarder beats him in the deck fight against his claws (it wins 100% in almost every setting), and the Gunline and the Firebrand beat him with Grapeshot into the claws. A search over doctrines is 27 seconds a style on the quick run, so a sweep of combinations is better scored on a fixed library of the best doctrines found at several settings, and confirmed with the real search at the end. The best doctrines found in this session are not kept: `--json` writes them (`styles[].finalists`).

**Findings for P3.2c.** The gate pace of check 5 still models one fight a Tide (D108) and uses the style that beats him most. Measured on the older combat of P3.1, with a win chance of about 45%, one fight a Tide put the median captain through his gate on Tide 10.4 and the last on Tide 15.2, and even with every fight won it needs Tides 5 to 10 for six captains, against the target of GAMEPLAY 2.5 (median by 7 to 9, nobody later than 12). Letting him fight every captain who reaches him, once a Tide each and whole every time, gave Tide 5.5 and Tide 8.6. The change was small: a boss is left out of `freeze` in `combatEncounter`, `restoreBoss` makes him whole after each fight (the same `applyStatline` the upkeep uses, and a `bossRecovers` event), and the pace model takes a number of fights a Tide. That work is on the branch `p3-2-tuning` (commit 25d6bba, on the combat before the Fighting Styles), not on main. The pool bug it also found is already fixed on main (D194).
