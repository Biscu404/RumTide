* To find a decision by its id, search for it (for example `grep -rn "| D142 " docs/DECISIONS.md docs/decisions`) and read only that part. Do not read a whole archive file to find one row.

## 2026-11-17: Battle viewer readability pass (sandbox stage 1)

Biscu tried the battle viewer and found it very hard to follow: ships jumped, bars jumped, and nothing said what each ship chose, which rule made it choose, or what happened when the two choices met. This pass changes the viewer only. No file under `supabase/` changed, so no outcome, rule, number or golden Tide can have moved. The engine needed no new helper: the battle record already holds the rule that fired (`RoundShipRecord.rule`), the Helm and Guns played, the deck pairing, the Hands lost, and every contest with its chance, the grapnel roll included. The new code is `sandbox/src/summary.ts` (the sentences and what the picture animates), `pacing.ts` (the timings), and the page in `main.tsx`. `words.ts` only gained three `export` keywords.

| Id | Call | Why |
|-|-|-|

## 2026-11-17: Old Shellback is beatable and not farmable, and P3.2 is locked (P3.2b, P3.2d)

Biscu lifted the pause on 17 November 2026. **All ten checks pass on the full run**, check 5 included. Three small rules for Old Shellback and one number: nothing outside `B.shellback` moved, no fight between two captains changed, and the golden Tide did not move (no ship in it meets him). D224 is not used.

**Why every number was a cliff (from battle traces, 300 fights a style).** Four of the six styles beat him with the same plan, "Hold, Grapeshot" every round. Nobody rammed, raked, boarded or used Hot Shot. The causes:

* **Grapeshot went through his shell at 80%, and his claws are his health as well as his weapon.** A Gunline that Holds at Close took 16 to 19 of his 52 claws a round (Steady Deck times the Close factor), so he reached the deck fight half dead, or was driven off before it.
* **The deck fight has no luck in it** (the finding of D198 again). Whoever had an edge won it every time: the Gunline by its Grapeshot, the Firebrand by Tinderbox, the Boarder by Fighters. The Skirmisher and the Rammer have no edge, so theirs was a coin flip.
* **He only ever Charges, and Repel beats Charge.** The Boarder's best doctrine was "IF their last Guns order was Charge THEN Repel", which won the pairing every round for 1.8 times the hit.
* So a fight fell into three buckets by the round he rears up (2, 3 or 4), and inside a bucket the result was fixed. The only losses of the Gunline, the Boarder and the Firebrand were the fights where he rears up in round 4 and his three broadsides add up to a sloop's 100 Hull. A number that moves a bucket moves a third of the fights at once, which is the cliff.
