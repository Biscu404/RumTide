# Ideas

## Parked

* NPC merchants and locals should fight like Smugglers (the Runner and the Coward before D123), not like fighting styles (D85). Helps every player who hunts a merchant. Goes into the NPC brain, Season 0 (P9).
* The escape roll could use the Rigging left, not the Sails stat, so a Crippled ship runs worse (D77). Helps combat feel. Goes into P3 tuning, Season 0.
* Fists could become a chosen Deck order, Take Them Alive: weaker blows, but every Hand it removes is wounded rather than lost, and a Capture won with it brings more volunteers. Helps the pillar "soft falls" and gives captors a reason to be gentle. Left out of the boarding spec because check 2 has no way to value it. Season 1 or later.
* Old Shellback could keep fighting every captain who reaches the Narrows in a Tide until one beats him, instead of one fight a Tide (D81, D108). Helps a group of friends who arrive together and now queue at the gate. Goes into P3 tuning, Season 0.
* A Trickster Fighting Style (False Colours): looks like a merchant to the enemy's doctrine, hides its rules from replays, and its first broadside does double damage. Helps the pillar that knowledge is power. Left out of the six styles (D123). Season 1.
* A Dread Fighting Style (Black Flag): every Hand the enemy loses forces a nerve check, and a failed check strikes their colours, as the real Jolly Roger was flown to make merchants give up. Needs a nerve rule that check 2 can value. Season 1.
